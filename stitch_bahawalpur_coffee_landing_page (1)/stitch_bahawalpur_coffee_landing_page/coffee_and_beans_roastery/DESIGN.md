## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an artisanal, third-wave specialty roastery and cafe culture positioned adjacent to the university community of Islamia University Bahawalpur (IUB). Drawing inspiration from international benchmark sanctuaries like % Arabica, Blue Bottle, and Ralph’s Coffee, the visual narrative balances refined academic poise with tactile craft honesty.

The stylistic direction blends **Minimalism** with **Tactile Warmth**:
- **Atmosphere:** Sun-drenched terra-cotta archways, smooth unglazed ceramic, textured oat paper, and deep espresso crema.
- **Personality:** Connoisseur-level precision, grounded warmth, intellectual tranquility, and quiet craftsmanship.
- **Visual Rhythm:** Spacious, deliberate whitespace punctuated by editorial-grade serifs for single-origin storytelling, paired with functional geometric grotesques for ordering workflows and flavor wheel notes.

## Colors

The palette reflects organic roast states, unbleached kraft textures, sun-baked clay, and shade-grown botanical canopies:

- **Primary (`#2B1810` - Roasted Espresso):** Deep, velvety dark roast tone commanding primary typography, focused outlines, and prominent primary action buttons.
- **Secondary (`#C86432` - Baked Terracotta):** Rich warm clay providing vibrant sensory accents, interactive callouts, active indicators, and tasting profile highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#5A6E58` - Botanical Sage):** Muted shade-tree foliage used for roast processing indicators (washed, natural), dietary tags, and organic certifications.
- **Neutral (`#F7F3EB` - Oat Cream / Kraft):** A tactile parchment-inspired neutral that serves as the root surface layer, eliminating harsh stark whites in favor of ceramic and raw paper warmth.
- **Support Tones:** A toasted amber (`#D99B4B`) for extraction timing and bean origin highlights, with crisp deep border accents (`#E6DFD3`).

## Typography

The type scale combines intellectual, literary heritage with modernist coffee menu discipline:

- **EB Garamond (Display & Headlines):** Infuses heritage and artisanal gravity into hero statements, roastery origins, and harvest stories. Features refined classical proportions and graceful serifs reminiscent of boutique European roasteries and timeless academic publishing.
- **DM Sans (Body, Navigation & Technical Labels):** Clean, open geometric proportions ensure razor-sharp legibility across digital menu boards, grind-size selectors, sensory descriptors, and rapid checkout experiences on compact handheld devices.
- **Uppercase Hierarchy:** Micro-labels, tasting notes tags, and pricing units rely on tracking (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) in medium-to-bold weights to evoke architectural cafe signage.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a responsive **fluid grid** calibrated to 12 columns on desktop (`1200px+`), 8 columns on tablet (`768px - 1199px`), and 4 columns on mobile (`<768px`).

- **Outer Margins:** Wide margins (`4rem` desktop, `1.25rem` mobile) frame content like fine print on stationery.
- **Card and Item Grids:** Structured 2-column and 3-column rhythms for coffee beans and drink menus, expanding to generous asymmetrical arrangements for roastery journal entries and seasonal origin highlights.
- **Vertical Flow:** Ample sectional breathing room (`space-xl`) isolates menu tiers, creating an unhurried, intentional dining and ordering experience.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids high-tech glowing dropshadows, relying instead on **tactile tonal layering** and **warm, low-opacity ambient shadows**:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base canvas: `#F7F3EB` (Oat parchment).
  - Elevated containers & cards: `#FFFFFF` or `#FDFCFA` (Porcelain cardstock).
  - Recessed interactive wells (e.g., quantity selectors, search fields): `#EFE9DC`.
- **Shadow Philosophy:** Shadows emulate soft morning daylight filtering through cafe blinds. Cast shadows use the roasted espresso hue rather than harsh black:
  - Low elevation (Cards, Chips): `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(43, 24, 16, 0.06)`.
  - Raised elevation (Dropdowns, Floating Cart Bar, Modals): `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(43, 24, 16, 0.12), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(43, 24, 16, 0.04)`.
- **Dividers & Borders:** Subtle, thin boundaries (`1px solid #E6DFD3`) mimicking delicate bookbinding and debossed packaging labels.

## Shapes

The geometry uses a restrained, architectural **Soft (`1`)** approach with `4px` (`0.25rem`) base radii, scaling up to `8px` (`0.5rem`) on cards and `12px` (`0.75rem`) on modal sheets.

- **Edge Quality:** Intentionally crisp and structured rather than bubbly or toy-like, evoking architectural joinery, stamped craft labels, and premium packaging boxes.
- **Exceptions:** Pill profiles (`9999px`) are reserved exclusively for circular flavor-tag chips, roast level badges, and interactive segment pills to contrast against clean square card frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep roasted espresso fill (`#2B1810`), crisp oat text (`#F7F3EB`), `4px` radius, uppercase tracking (`0.04em`), subtle hover transition to terracotta (`#C86432`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Parchment fill with a `1px` border in `#2B1810` or `#D6CABE`, text in `#2B1810`, shifting to cream wash on hover.
- **Accent Action (Order / Quick Add):** Terracotta fill (`#C86432`) with white text for decisive transactional moments.

### Chips & Flavor Badges
- Compact pill-shaped components for processing methods (e.g., "Natural Anaerobic", "Washed Geisha") and flavor descriptors (e.g., "Bergamot", "Stone Fruit").
- Built using tertiary sage (`#5A6E58`) or roasted terracotta tints (`rgba(200, 100, 50, 0.1)`) with matching deep-toned typography.

### Cards (Menu & Coffee Bags)
- Clean porcelain surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a delicate `1px` border (`#E6DFD3`).
- Generous padding (`space-lg`), high-contrast EB Garamond title, small uppercase origin metadata line (e.g., "HUEHUETENANGO // 1,800M"), and prominent pricing in DM Sans.

### Input Fields
- Recessed background (`#EFE9DC`), `4px` radius, thin `1px` border in `#DDCDBF`.
- On focus: crisp border in roasted espresso (`#2B1810`) with zero fuzzy glow, ensuring tactile clarity for table numbers, custom notes, and pickup details.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Square (checkbox) and circular (radio) controls rendered in terracotta (`#C86432`) when checked, with high-contrast oat indicators.

### Specialized Cafe Components
- **Tasting Notes Matrix:** A horizontal segmented badge strip detailing Acidity, Body, Sweetness, and Roast Level.
- **Brew Method Selector:** Minimal outlined icon tiles (Espresso, V60, Chemex, Aeropress, Cold Drip) with subtle active fills in warm oat cream.
