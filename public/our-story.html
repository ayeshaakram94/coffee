Thermodynamics Profile

The Roast Curve Architecture

Every batch goes through a deliberate 10-minute curve: Charge temperature at 205°C, turning point at 1:18, followed by yellowing at 4:30. We regulate airflow to keep the Rate-of-Rise (RoR) smoothly descending until the First Crack initiates, preserving delicate fruit acids.

Nordic Roast Philosophy

Light Scandinavian Brightness

Zero baked flavors, zero ash notes. Roasting with high kinetic energy early in the cycle keeps organic acids intact, highlighting stone fruit and lemon tea notes native to East African heirloom crops.

Bahawalpur Adaptation: Cold drip towers yielding slow 14-hour Kyoto-style extractions served over carved crystal ice spheres.

Extraction Science

The Barista as Scientist: Calibrated to the tenth of a gram.

Our team is trained under Specialty Coffee Association (SCA) principles. We test extraction yield daily, tracking how ambient Bahawalpur humidity and seasonal shifts impact particle distribution and water chemistry.

Metric 01

1.35% – 1.45%

TDS Refractometry: We test Total Dissolved Solids on our espresso yields daily to guarantee uniform concentration and eliminate astringency.

Mineral Architecture: Triple-stage reverse osmosis water re-mineralized with targeted ratios of magnesium and calcium ions to extract origin terroir.
