Extraction Fundamentals

Grind Size & Recipe Cheat Sheet

Even the finest specialty coffee degrades if under-extracted or ground coarsely for high-pressure shots. Follow our calibrated ratios.

coffee Hario V60

Grind Texture: Medium-Fine (Sea Salt)

Ratio: 1:16 (15g Coffee / 240g Water)

Water Temp: 92°C – 94°C

Drawdown Time: 2:45 – 3:15 min

Best paired with: Ethiopia & Kenya Lots
local_cafe Espresso Bar

Grind Texture: Fine (Powdered Flour feel)

Ratio: 1:2 (18g Dose / 36g Liquid)

Water Temp: 93.5°C

Shot Window: 27 – 31 seconds

Best paired with: Abbasia Blend & Sumatra
science AeroPress

Grind Texture: Medium (Granulated Sugar)

Ratio: 1:14 (17g Coffee / 240g Water)

Water Temp: 88°C – 90°C

Steep Time: 1:45 min + 30s press

Best paired with: Colombia Huila Anaerobic
filter_alt French Press
